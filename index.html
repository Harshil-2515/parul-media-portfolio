It started with a meme.

What began as a couple of meme pages turned into my first real lesson in social media. I spent hours creating posts, following trends and figuring out what makes people laugh, relate and share.

Later, I started experimenting with content on my personal Instagram, where several reels went past a million views and taught me how reels, trends and visual storytelling work together. Somewhere along the way, creating content became more than just a hobby.
